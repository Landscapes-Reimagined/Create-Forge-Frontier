# **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.5.3 Update**  
### *Foundation Refinement*

## Introduction
Version **2.5.3** of *Create - Forge Frontier* represents a major refinement update focused on stability, performance, and long-term maintainability as the pack transitions fully to **Create v6.0.8**. This update continues the ongoing effort to streamline progression, modernize automation pathways, and remove legacy systems that no longer align with the pack’s design philosophy.

Alongside extensive mod updates and removals, this release introduces new utility and performance-focused mods, significant quest restructuring, and deep KubeJS-driven recipe and data changes. Together, these improvements reinforce Forge Frontier’s identity as a tightly integrated, Create-centric experience built for both technical depth and long-term playability.

---

## Overview
The 2.5.3 update delivers a broad set of improvements across nearly every core system in the pack. Several outdated or incompatible mods have been removed to ensure cross-loader stability, while new additions focus on performance optimization, quality-of-life enhancements, and late-game automation expansion.

Quest content has been reorganized and refined to provide clearer progression paths, reduce redundancy, and improve onboarding through a new quest book distribution system. Internally, extensive KubeJS scripting updates rework recipes, tags, and automation loops to better align with Create, Dreams and Desires, Metallurgy, Mega Cells, and Powah, ensuring consistent progression and automation compatibility throughout the pack.

Finally, asset, data, and configuration updates polish the overall experience, improving visuals, recipe consistency, and performance tuning. Version 2.5.3 serves as a stabilization milestone that prepares *Create - Forge Frontier* for future content expansions while delivering a more cohesive and reliable experience in the present.

## Mod Additions
- [Fast Furnace](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fastfurnace): Improves server performance by optimizing furnace-related blocks through intelligent recipe caching, significantly reducing TPS impact in large modpacks.
- [Fast Workbench](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fastworkbench): Enhances crafting performance by caching recipe lookups and reducing unnecessary recalculations, resulting in smoother crafting and improved stability.
- [Gadgets Against Grind](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/gag): Adds a collection of balanced utility items designed to reduce repetitive gameplay tasks while maintaining a vanilla-friendly progression feel.
- [Mega Cells](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mega-cells): Expands Applied Energistics 2 with high-capacity storage tiers and a dedicated progression path, offering advanced storage solutions for late-game automation.
- [Pipe Connector](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pipe-connector): Simplifies pipe placement by allowing underground routing and precise node control, eliminating the need for disruptive excavation during infrastructure setup. 
- [Not Enough Recipe Books](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/notenoughrecipebook): Completely removes the vanilla recipe book system to reduce data bloat, improve performance, and speed up client-server synchronization in large modpacks.
- [Entangled](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/entangled): Enables instant interaction between linked blocks, allowing seamless transfer of items, fluids, and energy while helping conceal complex cabling setups.
- [JEI Copy Recipe Json](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei-copy-recipe-json): Adds a developer-focused JEI feature that allows copying full recipe JSON data to the clipboard with a single keybind.
- [Measurements](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/measurements): Introduces a tape measure tool for accurately measuring distances between blocks to assist with precise building and automation layouts.
- [Compact Machines](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/compact-machines): Allows complex contraptions to be built inside compact dimensions, reducing world clutter while supporting advanced automation designs.
- [Flerovium](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/flerovium): Significantly improves rendering performance by optimizing item, entity, and particle rendering through advanced culling and math optimizations.
- [FTB Quests Freeze Fix](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-quests-freeze-fix): Prevents in-game freezes by pre-caching FTB Quests data during startup, improving responsiveness when opening the quest book.
- [Lucent](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lucent): Provides configurable dynamic lighting with a focus on balancing visual quality and performance impact.
- [Bits N Bobs](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-bits-n-bobs): Adds small, high-quality decorative and functional components that integrate closely with Create to expand building and mechanical design options.

## Quest Updates & Additions  
- **Basic Storage Chapter**: Removed outdated quest rewards and made copper-tier quests optional to better support flexible progression paths.
- **Create Connected Chapter**: Added new quests, *Bulk Belching* and *Conduit Channeling*, and reworked catalyst descriptions to follow a unified thematic structure.
- **Create Forge Frontier Chapter**: Introduced a new chapter title and pack introduction, added a dedicated Issues and Suggestions quest linking to the universal GitHub repository, and rewrote the introduction to remove AI-generated content.
- **End Remaster Chapter**: Made all Eye-related quests optional, reformatted dependencies accordingly, and updated the Chromatic Compound reward to use the Dreams and Desires variant.
- **FTB Info Chapter**: Merged into the Informational Chapter Group and combined with Quality of Life content for better organization.
- **General Progression Chapter Group**: Renamed to *Exploration Progression* and condensed Overworld, Nether, Otherside, and End progression into a single chapter. Moved Cataclysm and overall progression chapters into this group.
- **Hidden Chapters**: Removed all unused hidden chapters to reduce clutter and improve maintainability.
- **Informational Chapter Group**: Consolidated Armor and Tools chapters into a single chapter, merged Artifacts and Relics into one chapter, and moved the hidden Cognition chapter into the Enchanting chapter. Additional quests will be added in future updates.
- **M0nkeyPr0grammer’s Quests**: Removed all AI-generated content from quest descriptions to ensure consistency and quality.
- **Mob Grinding Utils Chapter**: Updated the Chromatic Compound reward to use the Dreams and Desires variant.
- **Space Progression Chapter Group (formerly Ad Astra Progression)**: Renamed and condensed all planetary quests into a single chapter rather than a grouped structure.
- **Structure Exploration Chapter**: Condensed structure-related content into three chapters: Archeology Structures, Create Structures, and Other Structures.
