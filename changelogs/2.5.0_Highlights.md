# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.5.0**  
**Release Date:** October 7th, 2025  

# ⚠️ *Major Update – Progression & Performance Overhaul*  

### 📍 **Description**  
Version 2.5.0 is a **major milestone release** for *Create – Forge Frontier*, delivering the largest progression rework since launch. This update consolidates the modpack around Create’s core mechanics, replaces outdated systems, and introduces a wealth of new tools, structures, and performance improvements. Players can expect smoother gameplay, tighter progression, and a more immersive Create-centered experience across both Overworld and space exploration.  

⚠️ **Note:** Due to extensive changes to space-related structures and progression, we recommend **resetting space exploration dimensions** (planets, moons, etc.) to ensure access to new content.  

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.  

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**  

- ✅ Added **20+ new mods**, including **Create Metallurgy, Create: Ender Transmission, Create: Better Motors, Steam ’n’ Rails,** and more  
- ❌ Removed **Tinkers’ Construct**, **Pipez**, **EMI**, and other systems that conflicted with progression or performance goals  
- 🔄 Introduced **Pretty Pipes (with fluids addon)** as the new lightweight and modular item/fluid transport system  
- 🎯 Overhauled **progression balance**, integrating **Apotheosis** and **Metallurgy** to replace Tinkers’ Construct and deepen late-game customization  
- 🌍 Major **worldgen and structure updates** across Cataclysm, Incendium, and Ad Astra dimensions (recommended reset for space dimensions)  
- 📦 Massive **KubeJS script reworks**, covering resource processing, rocket recipes, new alloys, sequenced assemblies, and progression-locked systems  
- 🧩 New and refined quests, including **Cataclysm progression** and **Create Ender Transmission**  
- 🎨 Resource pack additions for improved UI and Create-style textures across third-party content  
- ⚙️ Updated UI/config: Drippy Loading Screen, FancyMenu, Explorer’s Compass, and Item Obliterator all reworked for better usability
