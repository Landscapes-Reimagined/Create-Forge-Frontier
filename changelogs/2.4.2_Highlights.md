# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.4.2**  
**Release Date:** July 3rd, 2025

# ⚠️ *Hotfix Patch – Performance Tweaks*

### 📍 **Description**
Version 2.4.2 is a focused **hotfix and optimization update**, targeting lag reduction and stability improvements for factory-scale gameplay. This patch removes underperforming or redundant mods, refines scripting for better backend behavior, and introduces highly specialized performance tools. The result is a noticeably smoother experience—especially for mid- to late-game players running complex Create contraptions or large world automation.

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**

- ✅ Added **Radium Reforged**, **Mobtimizations**, **BadOptimizations**, and more to boost client/server performance  
- ❌ Removed **Sound Physics**, **Model Gap Fix**, and other problematic or low-impact mods  
- 🎯 Improved stability and FPS in Create-heavy factories and redstone areas  
- 📦 Major script tweaks: washing salt, fixing compressed blocks, AE2 balance updates  
- 🧩 Refined quest flow for Lost Debris Vents and fluid recipes  
- 🎨 UI updates to **FancyMenu** and **Drippy Loading Screen** for version cohesion
