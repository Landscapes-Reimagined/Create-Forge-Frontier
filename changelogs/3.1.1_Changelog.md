# **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 3.1.1**
### *The Final Frontier*

### **Introduction**

Version **3.1.1** is a minor maintenance update following the release of Version **3.1.0**, focused on improving compatibility and overall pack performance through two targeted mod updates.

This release does not introduce any major progression or gameplay changes. Instead, it builds upon the systems and content introduced in Version **3.1.0** while providing additional refinements to ensure a smoother and more reliable experience.

---

### **Overview**

Version **3.1.1** includes two minor mod updates addressing compatibility and performance within the pack.

All major features introduced in **Version 3.1.0**, including the return of Applied Energistics 2, expanded Create integrations, the updated Create: Enchantment Industry integration, and associated progression changes, remain unchanged.

This update is fully focused on maintenance and does not alter the intended progression or gameplay experience established in Version **3.1.0**.
