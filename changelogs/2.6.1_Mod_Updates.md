# **Modpack Mod Updates: Version 2.6.1**

## **Introduction**
Version 2.6.1 focuses on improving stability, performance, and long-term compatibility following the Create v6.0.8 upgrade. This update includes targeted mod updates, library upgrades, and internal improvements to ensure a smoother gameplay experience while laying the groundwork for future content development.

---

## **Overview**
This release prioritizes backend reliability and quality-of-life improvements rather than introducing major new systems. Several core mods have been updated to resolve known issues, improve performance, and maintain compatibility across the modpack ecosystem.
