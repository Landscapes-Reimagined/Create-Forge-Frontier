# **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.6.0**  
**Release Date:** January 1st, 2026  

# *Foundation Refinement*  

### **Description**
Version **2.6.0** focuses on **stability, performance, and long-term maintainability** as *Create - Forge Frontier* fully transitions to **Create v6.0.8**.  
This update refines quest progression, modernizes automation systems, and removes outdated or incompatible content to deliver a more cohesive and reliable Create-centric experience.

Version 2.6.0 also serves as a **foundational preparation update** for the upcoming **v3.0 release**, which will introduce the final planet and conclude the pack’s progression arc. All changes and additions in this update are designed to align systems, balance progression, and ensure readiness for the final content expansion.

> **Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.
 
---

### **Key Highlights**
- **Create v6.0.8 Integration**: The pack has fully transitioned to Create v6.0.8, with extensive compatibility updates and removals of legacy or incompatible mods to ensure long-term stability.
- **Major Stability and Performance Improvements**: Numerous performance-focused mods have been added and updated, significantly reducing TPS impact, rendering overhead, and in-game freezes in large modded environments.
- **Quest System Refinement**: Quest chapters have been reorganized, condensed, and renamed to provide clearer progression, improved onboarding, and reduced redundancy across all dimensions.
- **Expanded Automation and Progression Support**: Deep KubeJS-driven recipe and scripting changes align automation paths across Create, Metallurgy, Mega Cells, Powah, and Dreams and Desires for consistent late-game progression.
- **New Utility and Quality-of-Life Mods**: Several new mods have been introduced to streamline building, automation, storage, and development workflows while maintaining a balanced, vanilla-friendly experience.
- **Cleanup of Outdated and Incompatible Content**: Mods relying on embedded Fabric APIs or no longer maintained have been removed to improve cross-loader compatibility and reduce instability.
- **Improved Visual and Asset Consistency**: Asset, texture, and model updates ensure consistent visual styling across new blocks, items, and processors introduced in this update.
- **Preparation for Future Content Updates**: Version 2.6.0 serves as a foundational refinement release, establishing a stable and maintainable baseline for upcoming feature-focused updates.
