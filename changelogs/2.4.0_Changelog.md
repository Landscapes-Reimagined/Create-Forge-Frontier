# 🛠️ **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.4.0 Update**  
### *Redefining Automation, Exploration, and Innovation in the Skies and Beyond.*

We're excited to unveil **Version 2.4.0** of **Create - Forge Frontier**, a feature-packed update focused on refining progression, expanding compatibility, and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with the Create ecosystem. This release introduces powerful new tools, major questline enhancements, fluid system reworks, and a revamped vent automation experience—all designed to elevate your modded Minecraft journey.

---

## 🆕️ **Mod Additions**

- 🔧 **[EMI: Create Schematics](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/emi-create-schematics)**  
  Adds support for Create schematics directly within the EMI interface, streamlining automation and blueprint planning.

- 🧠 **[Applied Schematicannon](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/applied-schematicannon)**  
  Integrates Create's Schematicannon with AE2, enabling automated building via stored patterns and autocrafting logic.

- 🚤️ **[Create Stock Bridge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-stock-bridge)**  
  Adds an AE Stock Bridge that links the two storage systems together. Items in the Create Stock system show up as craftable items in AE. Create can request stored items from AE through the bridge with a packager connected.

- 🍞 **[Create: Food](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-food)**  
  Introduces delicious, Create-compatible food production systems for culinary creativity.

- 💎 **[Create: Vibrant Vaults](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-vibrant-vaults)**  
  Adds richly designed vault structures filled with puzzles, traps, and treasure to explore using Create mechanics.

- 🏞️ **[Every Compat (Stone Zone)](https://legacy.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/stone-zone)**  
  Expands decorative and functional block variants across mods, enhancing terrain integration and building options.

- 🌋 **[Create: Resource Vents](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-resource-vents)**  
  A complete overhaul of vent automation, replacing Molten Vents with a far more advanced and immersive resource system.

- 🎧 **[Cable Facades](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cable-facades)**  
  Allows you to disguise and decorate cable networks with a variety of facades—perfect for aesthetic, high-tech builds.

---

## ❌ **Mod Removals**

- **Create Factory Logistics** – Removed due to persistent compatibility issues with Create and its addons.  
- **Create Molten Vents** – Retired in favor of the vastly superior **Create: Resource Vents** system.
