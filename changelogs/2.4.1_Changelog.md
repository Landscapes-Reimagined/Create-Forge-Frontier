# 🛠️ **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.4.1 Update**  
### *Hotfix Patch – Shader Compatibility & Vent Improvements*

The **v2.4.1 Hotfix Update** focuses on resolving key performance and compatibility issues following the release of 2.4.0. This patch introduces official shader rendering support with **Iris Flywheel Compat**, improves **Mechanical Extruder** integration with **Create: Resource Vents**, and delivers critical updates to quest progression and backend scripting. 

While smaller in scale, this update is crucial for a smoother gameplay experience—especially for shader users and late-game automation setups.

> ⚠️ **Reminder:** As with any update, we recommend **backing up your world** before applying the patch.

---

## 🆕️ **Mod Additions**

- 🔧 **[Iris & Oculus Flywheel Compat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/iris-flywheel-compat)**  
  Bridges the gap between Iris Shaders and Flywheel, enabling full compatibility between Minecraft's modern shader rendering and advanced modded rendering systems like those used in Create. Perfect for players who want both stunning visuals and smooth mod performance.

---

## 🔥 **Why These Changes?**

With more players using shaders, this update ensures **visual enhancements don’t conflict with performance-heavy mods like Create**. The hotfix also improves **Resource Vent automation logic**, streamlining one of the most powerful endgame mechanics in Forge Frontier.
