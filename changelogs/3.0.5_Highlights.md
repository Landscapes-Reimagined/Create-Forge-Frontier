Version **3.0.5** is the fifth maintenance update following *The Final Frontier*. This release focuses on bug fixes, compatibility improvements, quest corrections, and updated mods without changing progression or requiring a new world.

---

## **Overview**
Version **3.0.5** builds upon the foundation established in **Version 3.0.0**, improving stability, compatibility, and overall polish while maintaining full world compatibility with existing **3.0.0** saves.

---

## **Key Highlights**
- Removed **FTB Backups** due to world generation issues that could revert terrain and player-built structures.
- Increased Chromatic Compound availability and added missing loot tables for Infa Blocks and Rubber Saplings.
- Added a custom Create Mixer recipe for Blaze Powder and improved compatibility through missing item tag fixes.
- Rebalanced Create Resource Vent duplication recipes and added custom mold recipes for Create Diesel Generators to improve progression and recipe compatibility.
- Updated a few mods to improve overall stability and compatibility.
- Continued refining the systems introduced in **[v3.0.0 Highlights](https://github.com/Landscapes-Reimagined/Create-Forge-Frontier/blob/main/changelogs/3.0.0_Highlights.md)**.
