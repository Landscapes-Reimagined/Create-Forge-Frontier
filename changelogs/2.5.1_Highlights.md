# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.5.1**  
**Release Date:** October 11th, 2025  

# ⚙️ *Stability & Content Refinement Update*  

### 📍 **Description**  
Version 2.5.1 is a **refinement and maintenance update** for *Create – Forge Frontier*, focusing on **stability, balance, and improved gameplay flow** following the major v2.5.0 overhaul.  
This patch fine-tunes progression, introduces new cross-dimensional storage systems, and improves visual consistency across Create-related content.  

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.  

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**  

- ✅ Added **EnderTanks**, **EnderChests**, and **ShetiPhianCore** to replace the deprecated *Create: Ender Transmission* system  
- 🧭 Introduced **Ender Storage Quests**, expanding mid-game storage progression  
- 🚀 Reworked **Forge Rocket Tank** quests and recipes to utilize the new Ender storage system  
- 🧰 Adjusted **Create Addons** questline, removing outdated Ender Transmission objectives  
- ⚒️ Added the **Echo Shard Block** and new **Cataclysm Eye visuals** for improved aesthetic integration  
- 🎨 Enhanced **textures and GUIs** for a cleaner, more polished Create-style experience  
- 🔄 General **balance, recipe, and tag refinements** across Create systems for smoother progression  
- ⚙️ Minor **performance and compatibility improvements** to maintain a stable experience
- ☠️ Removed Herobrine
