## Mod Additions
- [Simple Magnets](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simple-magnets): Simple Magnets adds Magnets that pickup Items and Experience around the player! Magnets can be configured with a whitelist or blacklist and have up to an 11 blocks range! Magnets can also be used in Baubles or Curios slots! Magnets can also be toggled with a key bind!
