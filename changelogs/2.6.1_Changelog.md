# **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.6.1 Update**
### *Hotfix Patch*

## **Introduction**
Version 2.6.1 is a hotfix update focused on improving stability, refining automation balance, and resolving issues introduced during recent system changes. This release ensures a smoother progression experience while maintaining compatibility with Create v6.0.8.

---

## **Overview**
This update introduces a small quality-of-life mod addition, balances power generation systems, and refines KubeJS scripts to better align with the intended progression curve. While modest in scope, these changes improve both early-game flow and long-term automation reliability.

---

## **Mod Additions**
- **[Simple Magnets](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simple-magnets)**  
  Adds configurable item and experience magnets with adjustable range, whitelist and blacklist support, Curios compatibility, and keybind toggles for improved inventory management.

## **KubeJS & Script Changes**
### **Server Script Changes**
- Added a new **Raw Uraninite Mixing Recipe**, allowing earlier access to Powah automation
- Removed an unintended **Terracotta to Quartz** recipe chain that bypassed intended progression

---

## **Configuration Updates**
- **Powah**: Reduced generation output for Nitro-tier Magmators and Furnators to improve balance
- **JEI**: Updated ingredient sorting, recipe categories, and blacklist entries to reflect recent changes

---

## **Closing Notes**
Version 2.6.1 reinforces Forge Frontier’s focus on stable automation, structured progression, and long-term maintainability as development continues toward the final major content update.
