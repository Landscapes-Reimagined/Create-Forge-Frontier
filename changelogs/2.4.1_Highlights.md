# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.4.1**  
**Release Date:** June 25th, 2025

# ⚠️ *Hotfix Patch – Shader Support & Vent Tweaks*

### 📍 **Description**
The **v2.4.1 Hotfix** enhances visual compatibility for shader users, refines vent automation, and polishes quest structure introduced in the 2.4.0 update. This patch is designed to improve performance, streamline late-game automation, and maintain full immersion for players using Create with shaders like Iris and Oculus.

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**

- 🧩 **Shader Support Added** – Now compatible with **Iris & Flywheel**, ensuring smooth visuals and Create rendering with shaders.
- 🔧 **Mechanical Extruder Compatibility** – Resource Vents now support automated setups thanks to new config values.
- 📦 **Quest Improvements** – Polished storage quests, added tasks for Cable Anchors and Quartz Crystals, and improved layout.
- 🚀 **Ad Astra Balancing** – Updated rewards for Forge Alloy Parts to reflect adjusted compression recipes.
- 🛠 **New Conversion Recipes** – Shadow Steel and Refined Radiance now have shapeless crafting support.
- 🖥 **UI Sync** – FancyMenu and Drippy Loading Screen updated to v2.4.1 for consistency and polish.
