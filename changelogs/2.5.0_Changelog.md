# 🛠️ **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.5.0 Update**  
### *Codename: TBD*  

## 📖 Introduction  
Version **2.5.0** is one of the largest and most impactful updates to *Create – Forge Frontier* to date. This release strengthens the modpack’s identity as a Create-centric experience while improving long-term stability, performance, and thematic consistency.  

The update includes significant changes to mod integrations, resource handling, world generation, and backend systems. It also introduces new tools, features, and visual improvements that align more closely with the Create aesthetic and enhance overall gameplay.  

⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Due to substantial updates to **space-related world generation and structures**, we strongly recommend **resetting any space exploration dimensions** (e.g., planets, moons, and other off-world environments) when updating to Version 2.5.0. While not strictly required, this ensures access to new structures, loot, and progression paths without conflicts from older generation.  

As always, we also recommend creating a **backup of your world** before updating. While this update is designed to be stable and forward-compatible, safeguarding your progress is always the best practice.  

---

## 🔥 Why These Changes?  
The guiding vision of Version 2.5.0 is to refine *Forge Frontier* into a polished, scalable, and immersive Create-based progression pack. Key reasons for these adjustments include:  

- **Performance & Stability**: Mods like Pipez and EMI were replaced with lighter alternatives to significantly reduce lag and improve multiplayer performance.  
- **Thematic Cohesion**: New additions such as **Create Metallurgy** and the **Ornithopter Glider** reinforce the Create aesthetic, replacing content that felt disjointed or redundant.  
- **Balanced Progression**: Removing overlapping systems (e.g., Tinkers’ Construct) ensures a smoother gameplay loop, while introducing **Apotheosis** and **Metallurgy** deepens customization and late-game balance.  
- **Player Experience**: Visual improvements, new utility mods, refined recipes, and structural adjustments all aim to make the pack more intuitive and immersive without sacrificing challenge.  

Together, these changes push Forge Frontier forward as a stable and cohesive foundation for both current players and future expansions.

## ❌ Mod Removals  
- **EMI and Addons** – Replaced with **Base JEI** for improved performance and cleaner UI. EMI can be manually re-added if preferred without breaking compatibility.  
- **Create Songs and Speakers** – Removed due to lack of thematic alignment with the Create design vision.  
- **Hang Glider** – Replaced by **Create: Ornithopter Glider**, which integrates more seamlessly into the pack’s progression.  
- **Straw Statues** – Superseded by **Create QOL Statues**.  
- **Tinkers’ Construct and Addons** – Removed to reduce system overlap and replaced with **Apotheosis Gem Module** and **Create Metallurgy**.  
- **Mantle** – Removed as it was only a dependency of Tinkers’ Construct.  
- **Create: Food** – Temporarily removed due to recipe conflicts requiring major rework.  
- **Pipez** – Removed and replaced with **Pretty Pipes**, a more efficient solution that mitigates server lag and scales better for multiplayer and large factory setups.
- **Trash Cans** - Superseded by **Create QOL Trash Cans**.
