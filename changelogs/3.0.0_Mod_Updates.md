# **Modpack Mod Updates: Version 3.0.0**

## **Introduction**
This update represents the largest overhaul in Create - Forge Frontier's history, introducing significant additions to automation, exploration, combat, storage, and world generation while modernizing nearly every aspect of the modpack. Version 3.0.0 includes an extensive collection of new mods, major updates to existing content, library improvements, and the removal of outdated or incompatible components to deliver the most cohesive and polished Create-centric experience to date.
