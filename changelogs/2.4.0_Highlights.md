# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.4.0**  
**Release Date:** June 24th, 2025

# 🌐 **AE2 Beyond Update**

### 📍 **Description**
The **v2.4.0 "AE2 Beyond"** update pushes the boundaries of automation and compatibility across the Create ecosystem. This milestone release features a **complete overhaul of the vent system**, new **fluid mechanics**, and **expanded AE2 integration**, delivering an even deeper progression experience across space, tech, and logistics.

From powerful scripting enhancements and polished quests to immersive Create expansions and backend stability, Forge Frontier continues to redefine the modded skyblock frontier.

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.

> **⚠️ World Update Disclaimer:** To fully experience the new **Create: Resource Vents** system, we highly recommend **trimming your Overworld using [MCA Selector](https://github.com/Querz/mcaselector)** and **resetting all other dimensions**. This ensures new vents and structures properly generate and prevents legacy data from interfering with progression.

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**

- 🔧 **Create: Resource Vents** fully replaces the old molten vents with improved visuals, mechanics, and outputs.
- ✨ **Frostite and Lost Debris**: Two new vent types, fully integrated into questlines and recipes.
- 🎓 **Advanced AE2 Integration**: Applied Schematicannon enables new AE2/Create cross-functionality.
- 🍔 **Create: Food** and **Vibrant Vaults** offer flavorful new systems and puzzle-rich exploration content.
- ⚙️ **Expanded KubeJS scripts**: New molten fluids, dimension tags, recipes, and quest hooks.
- 🕵️ Refined quest chapters for storage, space, and Create content (Basic & Advanced Storage, Mercury, and more).
- 📏 **New Mods Added:** EMI: Create Schematics, Applied Schematicannon, Stock Bridge, Resource Vents, and more.
- ❌ Removed outdated mods: Factory Logistics and Molten Vents.
- 🎨 UI Overhaul: FancyMenu and Drippy Loading Screen now align with version 2.4.0.
