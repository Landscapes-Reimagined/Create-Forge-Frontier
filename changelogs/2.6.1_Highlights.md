# **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.6.1**
**Release Date:** January 7th, 2025  

### *Hotfix Patch*

## **Overview**
Version 2.6.1 is a targeted hotfix focused on stability, balance, and minor progression adjustments following the Create update. While no major systems were introduced, this patch refines existing mechanics and resolves issues identified after the previous release.

> **Reminder:** Always back up your world before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.

---

## **Key Highlights**
- Improved compatibility and stability with Create v6.0.8
- Removal of unintended or overpowered automation paths
- Minor quality-of-life improvements and internal fixes
