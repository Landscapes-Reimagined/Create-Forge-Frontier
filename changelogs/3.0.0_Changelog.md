# **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 3.0.0 Update**
### *The Final Frontier*

## **Introduction**

Version **3.0.0**, *The Final Frontier*, represents the largest content update ever released for *Create - Forge Frontier* and serves as the culmination of the pack's progression-focused design. Nearly every aspect of the modpack has been revisited, from storage and automation to combat, exploration, and planetary progression, creating a more cohesive experience centered around Create's mechanical gameplay. This milestone establishes the definitive vision for Forge Frontier while laying the groundwork for future maintenance and balance updates.

The update introduces a complete redesign of digital storage progression through the transition from Applied Energistics 2 to Refined Storage, expands planetary exploration with dozens of new structures and challenges, modernizes combat with Better Combat and Expanded Combat, and significantly increases Create integration across recipes, progression, and world generation. Combined with extensive KubeJS rewrites, datapack improvements, asset updates, and performance optimizations, Version **3.0.0** delivers the most polished and immersive Forge Frontier experience to date.

---

## **Overview**

Version **3.0.0** delivers sweeping improvements across nearly every major gameplay system. Legacy mods that no longer aligned with the pack's design philosophy have been removed and replaced with modern alternatives that better support automation, exploration, and long-term progression. Storage progression has been completely redesigned, world generation has been expanded with numerous Create-themed structures and improved planetary content, and combat has been transformed through new animation systems, equipment, and compatibility improvements.

Behind the scenes, hundreds of custom recipes, scripts, assets, datapacks, and configuration files have been updated to ensure a consistent Create-centric experience from the Overworld through the final stages of space exploration. Together, these changes establish Version **3.0.0** as the definitive edition of Create - Forge Frontier and the foundation for all future maintenance releases.
