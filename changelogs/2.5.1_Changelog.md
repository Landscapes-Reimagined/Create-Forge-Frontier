# 🛠️ **Create - Forge Frontier: Version 2.5.1 Update**  
### *Patch Revision*

## 📖 Introduction  
Version **2.5.1** is a **follow-up maintenance and refinement update** to *Create – Forge Frontier*.  
This release focuses on **stability improvements, bug fixes, and incremental adjustments** to ensure a smoother player experience following the major v2.5.0 milestone.  

As always, it’s recommended to **back up your world** before updating to avoid compatibility issues.  

---

## 🔧 Overview  
- Minor mod updates for performance and compatibility.  
- Adjusted recipes and tags to resolve known issues from 2.5.0.  
- General quest and texture refinements.  
- Ongoing optimization for Create automation and progression systems.  

---

## ❌ Mod Removals  
**Create: Ender Transmition** - Conflict with pipes causing client side crash, replaced with **EnderTanks** and **EnderChests** 

---

## ✅ Mod Additions  
- **ShetiPhianCore** – Core library required for EnderTanks and EnderChests, providing shared functionality and stability.
- **EnderTanks** – Adds color-coded, cross-dimensional fluid storage and transfer using Ender-link technology.
- **EnderChests** – Introduces color-linked, cross-dimensional item storage and transport for seamless logistics.
