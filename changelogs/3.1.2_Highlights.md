# **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 3.1.2**
**Release Date:** August 12th, 2026

### *The Final Frontier*

## **Overview**
Version **3.1.2** is a maintenance and compatibility update focused on polishing existing systems through targeted quest fixes, recipe corrections, configuration adjustments, and seven mod updates. This release improves consistency across several progression and automation systems while preserving the gameplay experience established in Version **3.1.0**.

---

## **Key Highlights**
- **Quest Fix**: Fixed the Alex's Caves Candicorn quest task to restore proper quest progression.
- **Storage Recipe Consistency**: Updated Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage Conversion Upgrade recipes to better match the remainder of their upgrade progression.
- **Create Mob Spawners Fixes**: Removed duplicate Tea spawning recipes and restored the original spawning recipes.
- **Ad Astra Compatibility**: Updated the Oil-to-Fuel recipe to use the Oil fluid tag for broader recipe compatibility.
- **Flux Networks Configuration**: Disabled Flux Networks chunkloading to keep chunkloading behavior more controlled across the pack.
- **Mod Updates**: Updated seven mods, including Create: Enchantment Industry, Create: Dragons Plus, Chunksending, Create Compatible Storage, Sophisticated Backpacks, Sophisticated Core, and Structurify.
- Continued refining the systems introduced in **[v3.1.0 Highlights](https://github.com/Landscapes-Reimagined/Create-Forge-Frontier/blob/main/changelogs/3.1.0_Highlights.md)**.
