## **Mod Additions**
- **[Tempad](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tempad)**: Adds a portable teleportation device that allows players to create and instantly travel between custom locations.
- **[Tempad With Shaders](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tempad-with-shaders)**: Restores Tempad portal rendering compatibility with shaders by providing an alternate portal effect that remains visible when shaders are enabled.

## Mod Removals
- **Create Teleporters**: Replaced with Temp Pad, and removed due to broken loot tables that could not be patch via datapacks.

---

## **Resource Pack Removals**
- **Create Teleporters Remastered Fix**: Removed due to mod being removed.
