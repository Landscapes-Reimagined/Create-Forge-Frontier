# 📆 **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 2.5.2**  
**Release Date:** October 21st, 2025  

# ⚙️ *Stability & Content Refinement Update*  

### 📍 **Description**  
Version 2.5.2 focuses on **stability, balance, and streamlined progression** across Forge Frontier’s complex Create ecosystem.  
This patch improves Metallurgy integration, questing logic, and performance consistency while addressing community-reported recipe and tag conflicts.  

> **🚨 Reminder:** Always **back up your world** before updating to prevent data loss or progression issues.  

---

### 🌟 **Key Highlights**  
- 🔧 **Improved Metallurgy Integration** – Reworked casting systems, added bronze compatibility, and introduced casing recipes.  
- 📦 **Questline Expansion** – Updated AE2, Cataclysm, and Resource Vent progression for smoother pacing *(by CtrlAltDyleted)*.  
- 🧱 **Fixed Block Tags** – Removed Pretty Pipes from Create’s wrenchable list to prevent accidental disassembly.  
- 🎨 **UI & Asset Enhancements** – Refined JEI order, improved tooltip accuracy, and updated internal resource identifiers.  
- 🗺️ **World Settings Restored** – Tectonic’s Y-level cap corrected back to 640 for proper 3D world generation.  
- 🧠 **Performance Updates** – Faster KubeJS initialization and optimized startup scripts reduce world load time.  
- ⚒️ **Bug Fixes** – Addressed missing metallurgy recipe sets and resolved recipe ID mismatches.  
- ☠️ Removed Herobrine
